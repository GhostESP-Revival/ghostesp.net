Banshee joystick cap.

A drop-on 3D-printable cap for the joystick on The Banshee. Designed by @tototo31.

Download the 3MF (Free) About The Banshee
Overview

What it does.

The Banshee's joystick ships bare. This cap presses onto the stick to give it a wider, softer top face, easier to grip and kinder to your thumb during long sessions. It's a one-piece print with no hardware and no supports.
